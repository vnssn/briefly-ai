## Project brief

Briefly helps students and small teams turn scattered meeting notes into clear summaries, decisions, action items, and unresolved questions. Gemini extracts structured information; users review the brief, track actions, and export Markdown. The idea addresses the gap between a meeting and knowing what to do next.
